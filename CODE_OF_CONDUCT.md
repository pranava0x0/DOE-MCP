# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to get something
right.

## What is expected

- Argue with the claim, not the person making it.
- Say what you actually verified and what you assumed. In a project whose
  whole point is provenance, "I checked this on this date" and "this looks
  right to me" are different statements and both are welcome — as long as
  they are labelled.
- Accept a correction without a fight, and offer one without a flourish.
- Assume good faith about the publishers this project reads. A confusing API,
  a stale catalog, and a WAF that drops scripted clients are all ordinary
  facts about running public infrastructure, not evidence anyone is
  incompetent. Describe them accurately and without editorialising.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, and unwelcome attention are
not acceptable in issues, pull requests, commits, or any other project space.
Neither is deliberately wasting contributors' time.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this, and
will explain why. Repeated or serious violations lead to a ban. Report
concerns privately to the maintainers; reports will be handled discreetly.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, rewritten to say what this
project actually cares about.
